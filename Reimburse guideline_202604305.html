Travel Reimbursement

Documents checklist:

  • Leave Application form (THE grant used for reimbursement must match leave application)
  • Invitation letter or conference notification (会议通知)
    • For invited visitors: If support provided, we need to obtain VP's approval and its screenshot is required by the finance

    ✈️ Flight:

    • Boarding passes (photo or original)
    • Chinese VAT invoice (remark need show itinerary, passenger info)
    • Online order page or booking confirmation email (showing price breakdown, passenger & itinerary)
    • Payment proof (if >10k RMB)

    🚆 Train:

    • Online order page or booking confirmation email (if online booking)
    • Train ticket invoice (only can be issued via official China Railway app 12306 or from the train station counter) – 12306 website | Guide on invoice

    🏨 Hotel:

    • Chinese VAT invoice
    • Stamped hotel folio showing price breakdown, dates, guest info
    • Online order or booking confirmation email if booked online

    🚗 Car ride to/from airport:

    • Electronic itinerary
    • Chinese VAT invoice
  • 1. Platforms like Didi do not count toll fee (usu. 17 RMB) in the total on the invoice. In cases where a toll fee is incurred and you need to reimburse it, please ask the driver for the separate invoice.
  • 2. To avoid the above problem, you can take a taxi and ask for an invoice from the driver when getting off the car.

  • Associate Professors and others: Economy-class flight and Second-class train

    🍱 Per diem allowance (meals & local transport)

    • Rate: Meal 100 RMB/day + Local transport 80 RMB/day.
    • Prerequisite: Invitation must explicitly state "no meals or local transport provided".

    📅 Per diem counting rules

    Trip duration (days)Allowance calculation
    ≤ 10 daysFull allowance for all days (meal + local transport).
    10–15 days10 days full allowance + extra days at half rate.
    ≥ 15 days10 days full + 5 days half allowance (remaining days no per diem).
    Per diem claimable only if traveler bears own meals & local transit. "No support" clause mandatory.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    • Q: Can I get reimbursed for the seat selection fee of my flight ?
      A: No, it is not reimbursable.
    • Q: What type of insurance can be reimbursed for my travel?
      A: The personal accident insurance can be reimbursed with a valid invoice.
    • Q:Can I reimburse the actual expenses for my car ride to/from the airport in the destination of my academic trip instead of claiming per diem?
      A: Yes, but please ensure you have a valid invoice/receipt and an itinerary which clearly shows the route between the airport and your accommodation.
